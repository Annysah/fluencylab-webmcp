Common questions

Is this a grammar checker?

No. Grammar is part of the feedback, but the main goal is clearer thinking, structure, and articulation.

Can I practice without speaking?

Yes. Speaking mode supports audio recording, but you can also type a structured transcript.

Do I need an account?

An account is useful for saving progress and history. The practice surface itself stays simple and focused.

Who is it for?

Students, job seekers, operators, founders, and professionals who need to communicate clearly under time pressure.

Terms of Use

Last updated: September 1, 2026

FluencyLab is a communication practice simulator that helps you rehearse writing and speaking under light time pressure. By using the app, you agree to use it for lawful practice, learning, and personal or professional development.

Training Feedback

Scores, suggestions, and improved sample answers are generated for practice only. They are not professional, academic, hiring, legal, or language-certification decisions. Use the feedback as guidance and apply your own judgment before relying on it in real situations.

Your Account

If you create an account, you are responsible for keeping your login details secure. You should only submit responses that you have the right to use, and you should avoid adding confidential workplace, client, medical, legal, or financial information.

Acceptable Use

  • Do not use FluencyLab to harass, deceive, impersonate, or harm others.
  • Do not attempt to disrupt, reverse engineer, or abuse the app or its authentication services.
  • Do not submit unlawful, highly sensitive, or confidential information that should not be processed by a training tool.

Availability

FluencyLab is provided as-is. Features may change as the product develops, and service availability may depend on browser support, Supabase authentication, microphone permissions, and network access.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

FluencyLab is designed for focused communication practice. This policy explains what the current app handles and what you should avoid entering while practicing.

Information You Provide

The app may collect the email address you use for sign in, your generated practice prompts, typed responses, feedback results, scores, and practice history. If you use speaking mode, your browser may record audio for playback during the session.

How Information Is Stored

Authentication is handled through Supabase. Practice history is stored locally in your browser for the signed-in user experience in this current version. Clearing browser storage or changing devices may remove locally stored practice history unless a backend database is added later.

Microphone Access

Speaking practice only uses the microphone after you grant browser permission. Recorded audio is used for local playback in the session. You can type a transcript instead if you do not want to use microphone recording.

How Information Is Used

  • To generate prompts and provide structured feedback.
  • To show progress, history, streaks, and score breakdowns.
  • To improve the usability and reliability of the training experience.

Your Choices

You can sign out at any time, clear your practice history inside the app, deny microphone access, or avoid submitting sensitive information. For account-related data, manage authentication through the connected Supabase project.
